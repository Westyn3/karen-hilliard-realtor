Lake Lanier, GA

North Georgia's
Lake Lifestyle

Thirty-eight thousand acres of water across Hall, Forsyth, Gwinnett, and Dawson counties, less than an hour from Atlanta. Lake Lanier is the reason many families move to North Georgia, and it's where Karen has lived for more than a decade.

The lake rewards local knowledge more than any market in the state. Dock permits belong to the property, water depth changes with the season, and each shore — Gainesville, Flowery Branch, Buford, Cumming, Dawsonville — has its own character and price.

  • ✓ Established gated communities: Harbour Point, Marina Bay, Chattahoochee CC
  • ✓ Dock permits, water depth & Corps of Engineers shoreline rules verified
  • ✓ South shore for the commute, north shore for deep water & privacy
  • ✓ Luxury lakefront estates well into seven figures

Luxury Market

Milton & Alpharetta, GA

Estates, Equestrian &
Top-Rated Schools

Milton's large-lot zoning preserves equestrian estates and gated golf communities minutes from Alpharetta's tech corridor, Avalon, and downtown. Together they form North Atlanta's benchmark luxury market.

Karen's Keller Williams office is in Alpharetta, and it's where many clients start — comparing a luxury home here against an estate in Milton, more house in Cumming, or a lake home on Lanier.

  • ✓ The Manor Golf & CC, White Columns, Crooked Creek, Windward
  • ✓ Equestrian estates along Birmingham Hwy & Freemanville Rd
  • ✓ Cambridge, Milton & Alpharetta high school clusters
  • ✓ True estates from $1.5M; luxury from $1M

Forsyth County

Cumming, GA

Georgia's Fastest-Growing
County — With a Shoreline

Nationally ranked Forsyth schools, deep new-construction inventory, the Cumming City Center, and Lake Lanier's southwest shore. Forsyth County has spent a decade among America's fastest-growing counties for good reason.

Karen's construction background is especially valuable here, where so much of the inventory is new or recently built, and the lake side of the county offers Forsyth schools with a boat slip.

  • ✓ Vickery, master-planned new construction & swim-tennis Cumming
  • ✓ Southwest Lake Lanier: Bald Ridge Marina & lake-access neighborhoods
  • ✓ Builder contract review & walkthroughs at every stage
  • ✓ Growing seven-figure market in South Forsyth

Mountain Living

Dawsonville · Dahlonega · Blue Ridge

Cabins, Views &
Mountain Towns

From Dawsonville, where GA-400 ends and Big Canoe begins, through Dahlonega's gold-rush square and wine country, to Blue Ridge and one of the Southeast's strongest cabin markets — the mountains are an hour or two from Atlanta.

Mountain property has its own due diligence: road access and grade, well and septic, seasonal versus year-round views, and realistic short-term rental projections. Karen evaluates all of it before an offer.

  • ✓ Big Canoe, Achasta, Chestatee & Lake Blue Ridge communities
  • ✓ Short-term rental cabins in Blue Ridge & the Aska Adventure Area
  • ✓ Vineyard-view estates around Dahlonega
  • ✓ Acreage, view lots & buildability evaluated like a builder
