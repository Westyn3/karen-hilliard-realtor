## 2. Lakefront Properties Require Specialized Knowledge

Buying a Lake Lanier home involves considerations you won't encounter with a typical residential property.

I help buyers understand important factors such as:

* **Dock permits:** Army Corps of Engineers regulations, permit transfers, and dock considerations.
* **Water depth:** Seasonal water fluctuations and accessibility.
* **Location:** Quiet coves versus busy boating areas.
* **Shoreline:** Walking distance, terrain, and accessibility to the dock.
* **Property values:** How waterfront features affect pricing and resale potential.

My goal is to help you make an informed decision and avoid costly surprises.
