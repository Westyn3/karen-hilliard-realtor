## Our 5-Step Senior Transition Plan

At The Legacy Team, we've developed a personalized approach to make downsizing and selling a longtime home easier for Gainesville families.

### 1. Start With a Personal Consultation

We meet with you and your family, if desired, to discuss your goals, timeline, home's current market value, and available options. There's never pressure to make a decision before you're ready.

### 2. Organize and Simplify

Downsizing often means sorting through years of belongings. We can connect you with professional organizers and other trusted resources to make the process more manageable.

### 3. Decide What to Keep, Sell, or Donate

From estate sale specialists to professionals who can help evaluate valuable belongings, we connect you with resources to simplify the process.

### 4. Prepare Your Home for Sale

Not every home needs expensive renovations before selling!

We help determine which improvements may increase your home's value and which expenses you can skip.

**We also have access to contractors who may be able to complete qualifying repairs with payment at closing.**

For homeowners who prefer not to make repairs, we can explore potential as-is and investor purchase options.

### 5. Market, Sell, and Coordinate Your Move

Our team handles strategic pricing, professional marketing, showings, negotiations, and contract-to-closing coordination.

Our goal is to help you achieve a strong sale price while reducing the stress of your transition.
