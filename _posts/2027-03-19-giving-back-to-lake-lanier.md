When people fall in love with Lake Lanier, they often ask how they can give something back. The good news is that there are plenty of ways, and many of them are easy.

**Join the Lake Lanier Association.** The [LLA](https://lakelanier.org/) is a nonprofit supported by about 4,500 members, working since 1966 for a clean, full, and safe Lanier. Membership funds the programs that benefit everyone on the lake. I'm a proud member, and I'd encourage other lake owners and lovers to join.

**Volunteer for Shore Sweep.** The annual cleanup is the largest on the lake. The 2025 event involved more than 1,200 volunteers and collected a record 128 tons of trash. It's a good way for families, neighbors, and businesses to spend a day on the water.

**Help test the water.** The LLA's Adopt-a-Lake program relies on volunteers who test 27 sites monthly. It requires access to a boat and some training, but it's a meaningful way to help.

**Take the Clean Lake Pledge.** The LLA's pledge lists simple actions that reduce your impact on water quality, from lawn care to how you maintain your dock.

**Pack in, pack out.** The easiest contribution: take your trash with you.

**Look after your own shoreline.** If you own waterfront property, shoreline stabilization and thoughtful landscaping help keep sediment out of the lake.
