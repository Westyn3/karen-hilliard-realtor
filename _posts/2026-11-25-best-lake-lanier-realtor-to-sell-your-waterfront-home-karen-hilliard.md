## 2. Pricing Lake Lanier Waterfront Homes Requires Specialized Knowledge

No two Lake Lanier properties are exactly alike.

A home with deep water, a permitted dock, and beautiful open-water views may appeal to a different buyer than a similar home tucked into a quiet cove.

When developing your pricing strategy, I evaluate:

* **Water depth and location:** Seasonal water levels, cove characteristics, and boating access.
* **Dock features:** Dock size, condition, permit status, and available amenities.
* **Lake views:** Open-water views, sunsets, and waterfront privacy.
* **Shoreline accessibility:** Terrain, steps, pathways, and distance to the dock.
* **Property condition:** Updates, renovations, outdoor living spaces, and overall presentation.
* **Comparable waterfront sales:** Recent Lake Lanier sales and current competition.

**Correct pricing is one of the most important decisions you'll make when selling your lake home.**

My goal is to position your property competitively from the beginning, attract qualified buyers, and help you achieve the strongest possible outcome.
