Type "top real estate agent Alpharetta" into Google and you'll get ads, paid rankings, and a hundred smiling headshots. None of it tells you who's right for *your* move. After years in this market, here are the questions I'd ask any agent — including me — before signing anything.

**"Where do you actually work?"** Alpharetta agents range from hyper-local specialists to metro generalists. If your search might stretch north — toward [Cumming](/cumming.html), the lake, or the mountains — you want an agent who genuinely knows that territory, not one who'll be learning it on your dime. My practice runs from Alpharetta and [Milton](/milton.html) up through Forsyth, Hall, and the North Georgia mountains, because that's the corridor my clients actually shop.

**"What happens when the inspection gets complicated?"** Anyone can show houses. The job is what happens under contract — negotiating repairs, reading a builder's agreement, knowing whether a foundation quote is real. I came to real estate from construction management, and it's the part of my background clients end up valuing most.

**"Will I work with you, or a team member I haven't met?"** Big teams hand you off. On The Legacy Team it's me and my daughter Britney — two Realtors, and you get both of us, every time.

**"Can I see your reviews — all of them?"** Don't take a website's word for it (including mine). Read the Google reviews directly. I hold a 5.0 across 99 of them, and I reply to every one, because that record *is* the interview.

**"What will you tell me that I don't want to hear?"** This one matters most. An agent who only agrees with you is a liability in a negotiation. Ask for an example of talking a client *out* of something.
