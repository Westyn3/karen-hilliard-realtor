Once April arrives, lake house shopping gets competitive. The buyers who do best are the ones who've already done the groundwork. If you want to be one of them, here's a checklist for the next few weeks.

**Get your financing in order.** Talk to a lender now about pre-approval and how lakefront or luxury purchases are underwritten. My [trusted vendor list](/trusted-vendors.html) is a good place to start.

**Narrow your communities.** Decide which areas fit how you want to live. My [community comparison](/blog/luxury-lake-lanier-communities-compared/) is a good starting point.

**Choose your water.** Cove or main channel? See [cove vs. main channel](/blog/cove-vs-main-channel-lake-lanier/).

**Know the lake calendar.** Winter pool runs through April 30, and summer pool begins May 1. See [winter pool vs. summer pool](/blog/lake-lanier-winter-pool-vs-summer-pool/). Tour in late winter if you can, because you'll see the real shoreline and have fewer people competing.

**Learn the dock questions.** The checklist in [dock permits, part two](/blog/lake-lanier-dock-permits-part-two/) will save you from expensive surprises.

**Line up your inspectors.** Home, dock, septic if applicable, and survey. Having names ready means you can move quickly when the right property appears.

**Decide how you'll use the house.** Full-time or part-time? That affects the location, the community, and the level of maintenance you'll want to manage.

**Set up alerts and talk to me.** New lake listings often go quickly in spring. I'll set up a search tailored to your criteria so you hear about properties as they come on the market.
