A growing share of my buyers don't live in Georgia yet. They're relocating for work, family, or a lake-and-mountains lifestyle they've been planning for years — and most of them buy their North Georgia home having visited only once or twice. Here's how that actually works.

**First: pick your geography, not your house.** North Georgia offers several very different lives within an hour of each other. [Alpharetta](/alpharetta.html) and Milton mean top schools, the tech corridor, and walkable town centers. [Cumming and Forsyth County](/cumming.html) stretch the dollar with nationally ranked schools. [Lake Lanier](/lake-lanier.html) is a lifestyle of its own — and the mountains around Blue Ridge and Dahlonega are another one entirely. The first thing I do with relocating buyers is a video call about how you actually live: commute, schools, water, quiet. That conversation eliminates half the map before you ever book a flight.

**Then: shop remotely, seriously.** I do live video walkthroughs of every candidate home — not a polished highlight reel, but the version where you tell me to open the closet, walk the backyard, and show you the road noise. Between visits, you'll see neighborhoods by drone, school data, and honest commute times. When you do fly in, we see a shortlist that's already been vetted, not a scavenger hunt.

**Close from anywhere.** Georgia closings can be handled with remote and mail-away arrangements, and I coordinate inspectors, the lender, and the closing attorney so the process doesn't require you to be here. Plenty of my clients get their keys the day they arrive.

**One honest warning:** relocation buyers get burned when they buy the *house* photos and ignore the *location* facts. My job is to be your eyes on the ground — including telling you when the beautiful listing backs up to a highway.
