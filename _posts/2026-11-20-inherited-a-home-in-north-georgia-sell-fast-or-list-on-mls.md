## 1. You Have Two Main Options for Selling an Inherited Home

One of the first questions families ask is, "Should we fix up the house and list it, or sell it as-is?"

The answer depends on the property's condition, your timeline, and your financial goals.

At The Legacy Team, we help you compare two potential selling strategies.

### Option 1: Sell Your Inherited Home Off-Market to an Investor

If you've inherited a property that needs repairs, contains years of belongings, or simply needs to be sold quickly, an off-market sale may be the right solution.

**We work with real estate investors who may be interested in purchasing inherited homes throughout Gainesville and North Georgia.**

Potential benefits include:

* Selling the property as-is without major renovations
* Avoiding the traditional listing and showing process
* Potentially reducing preparation and holding costs
* Exploring cash offers and flexible closing timelines
* Simplifying the sale of properties with deferred maintenance

This can be especially helpful when heirs live out of state, the home has been vacant, or family members prefer a faster, more straightforward sale.

**Our goal is to help you explore a convenient selling option without unnecessary stress.**

Investor offers are typically below fully marketed retail prices, so we'll help you understand the trade-offs before making a decision.

### Option 2: List Your Inherited Home on the MLS for Maximum Exposure

If maximizing your sale price is your priority, listing the property on the Multiple Listing Service (MLS) may be the better option.

Our team creates a customized marketing strategy designed to attract qualified buyers and position your property competitively.

Our marketing may include:

* Professional photography and property videos
* Strategic pricing based on current market conditions
* MLS exposure to buyers and real estate agents
* Marketing on Zillow and other major real estate websites
* Social media advertising and property reels
* Open houses and direct buyer outreach
* Professional negotiation and contract management

**More exposure can create greater competition among buyers and opportunities for stronger offers.**

We'll help you determine whether preparing the property for the open market could produce a better net return than selling directly to an investor.

## 2. What If the Inherited Home Needs Repairs?

Many inherited homes haven't been updated in years, and some require significant repairs.

You may be dealing with outdated kitchens, old flooring, deferred maintenance, or a property that needs a complete renovation.

The good news? **You don't necessarily have to renovate before selling.**

At The Legacy Team, we help families determine which improvements may increase the home's value and which expenses can be avoided.

We also have relationships with contractors who may offer payment-at-closing arrangements for qualifying projects.

If repairs don't make financial sense, we can explore an as-is sale through the MLS or potential investor buyers.

## 3. What Happens When Multiple Family Members Inherit a Property?

Selling an inherited property can become complicated when several heirs are involved.

Some family members may want to sell immediately, while others may want to keep the home or make improvements before listing.

Our team understands the importance of patience, communication, and helping families evaluate their options.

We can coordinate real estate discussions with the appropriate family members and work alongside estate attorneys when legal questions arise.

**Our goal is to make the real estate portion of the process as organized and stress-free as possible.**

## 4. Do You Have to Go Through Probate Before Selling an Inherited Home in Georgia?

Not every inherited property follows the same legal process.

Depending on how the home was titled, whether a will exists, and who has legal authority over the property, probate or other legal steps may be necessary before the sale can close.

We can help coordinate with your estate or probate attorney and title professionals to understand the requirements for moving forward.

If you're unsure where to begin, we can help connect you with professionals who handle Georgia estate and probate matters.

**You don't need to have every answer before contacting us.**
