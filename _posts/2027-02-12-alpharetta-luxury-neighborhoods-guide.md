Alpharetta is one of the most popular luxury markets in North Georgia, and it's more varied than many buyers realize. Here's how I explain the main options to clients. For the full overview, see my [Alpharetta page](/alpharetta.html).

**Windward: the benchmark.** Windward is the name most people think of first for Alpharetta luxury, with lake, golf, and marina amenities that give it a resort-like feel inside the city.

**Downtown Alpharetta and Avalon.** If you want to walk to restaurants and events, the newer luxury construction in and around downtown is the draw. It's a different lifestyle from a large-lot estate: more convenience, less acreage.

**The Milton border.** Estate communities along the edge of Alpharetta give you a quieter, more private setting while keeping you close to everything. If you want even more land, [Milton](/milton.html) is the next step up, with larger minimum lot sizes. I compare the two in [Milton vs. Alpharetta](/blog/milton-vs-alpharetta/).

**What I help buyers weigh:**
- *Lot size and privacy* versus walkability.
- *Schools.* The Alpharetta, Milton, and Cambridge high school clusters are consistently among Georgia's top-rated, but zoning varies by address, so I verify it for every home.
- *Commute.* GA-400 access matters.
- *Association rules and fees* in gated communities.
- *New construction vs. resale.* More in my [new construction guide](/blog/new-construction-cumming-forsyth/).
