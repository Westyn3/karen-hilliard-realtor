## Downsizing Is About More Than Selling Your House

For many homeowners, downsizing means leaving a property where they've lived for 20, 30 or even 40 years.

There may be decades of memories, furniture, belongings and family history tied to that home.

That's why our approach isn't simply putting a sign in the yard and selling your house.

We can help you develop a plan for the entire transition—from determining what your current home could sell for to deciding where you want to live next.

That can include preparing and marketing your current property, identifying repairs or improvements that make financial sense, coordinating contractors, finding your next home and helping coordinate the timing of your sale and purchase.

Our goal is to make the process easier, with **one call to get started**.

## Should You Buy First or Sell First When Downsizing?

This is one of the biggest questions homeowners ask us.

And there isn't one answer that works for everyone.

Your strategy depends on your home equity, financing, current market conditions, how quickly your existing property is likely to sell and whether you're purchasing an existing home or building new construction.

Before putting your home on the market, we can help answer questions such as:

**What is my house worth in today's market?**

**Should I renovate before selling?**

**Which repairs are actually worth doing?**

**Should I buy my next house before selling this one?**

**How do I coordinate the sale and purchase?**

**What happens to everything I don't want to take with me?**

**Would new construction be a better option?**

The earlier we start planning, the more options you may have.
