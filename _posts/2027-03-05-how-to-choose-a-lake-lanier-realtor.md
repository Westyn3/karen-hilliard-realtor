Choosing a realtor for a luxury lake home is one of the most important decisions you'll make, and the best way to judge an agent is to ask specific questions. Here are the ones I'd ask if I were hiring.

1. **How do you verify a dock permit?** The answer should involve the Corps of Engineers permit itself, not a verbal assurance. (My [dock permit questions](/blog/lake-lanier-dock-permits-part-two/) show what thorough looks like.)
2. **How do you check water depth?** A good answer includes seasonal levels. Full pool is 1,070 feet in winter and 1,071 in summer.
3. **Which communities do you know best, and what are their rules and fees?** Ask for specifics on at least two.
4. **Do you live on or near the lake?** Local knowledge shows. I've lived on Lanier for more than twelve years.
5. **Are you connected to the lake community?** I'm a member of the [Lake Lanier Association](https://lakelanier.org/), which works on water quality, shorelines, and advocacy.
6. **Who's on your team?** Lenders, inspectors, and other local professionals. Ask who they'd call; mine are on my [trusted vendor list](/trusted-vendors.html).
7. **How will you market my home (or find mine)?** For sellers, ask about drone and video. For buyers, ask how they find off-market and new listings.
8. **Can I talk to past clients?** Reviews tell you a lot. You can read [mine here](/reviews.html).

**Red flags:** vague answers about docks, not knowing the lake level, or treating a lake home like any other house.

**What a luxury lake specialist should give you:** honest advice, clear explanations, and someone who tells you when a beautiful property isn't right.
