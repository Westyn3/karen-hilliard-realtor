If you've never bought on a lake before, the process can feel unfamiliar. Here's how I walk my buyers through it, from first conversation to the keys in your hand.

**1. A conversation about how you want to live.** Full-time or weekend? Boat size? Gated community or private lot? Budget and timeline? This conversation shapes everything after it. See [New Year, New Lake Home](/blog/plan-your-lake-lanier-home-purchase-2027/) for a timeline.

**2. Financing.** Talk with a lender early about pre-approval and any considerations for lakefront properties.

**3. The search.** I set up a custom search and walk you through the [communities](/blog/luxury-lake-lanier-communities-compared/) that fit your goals. For remote buyers, I do live video tours. See my [relocation guide](/blog/relocating-to-north-georgia/).

**4. Showings.** When possible, we see properties from both land and water. We look at the cove, depth, and shoreline, and I check the lake level and seasonal depth for every property. (More in [cove vs. main channel](/blog/cove-vs-main-channel-lake-lanier/).)

**5. Due diligence on the water.** Dock permit, dock condition, depth in different seasons, and community rules. My [dock questions](/blog/lake-lanier-dock-permits-part-two/) lay out the checklist.

**6. The offer.** We write an offer with the right protections and contingencies for a lake purchase.

**7. Inspections.** Home, dock, and, where relevant, septic and survey. I can point you to local professionals from my [trusted list](/trusted-vendors.html).

**8. Closing.** I coordinate the lender, closing attorney, and other parties so the final steps go smoothly.

**9. After closing.** Move-in, introducing you to local vendors, and helping you get connected to the lake community, including the [Lake Lanier Association](https://lakelanier.org/).

Every purchase is a little different, but the goal is always the same: no surprises, and a home you'll love for years.
