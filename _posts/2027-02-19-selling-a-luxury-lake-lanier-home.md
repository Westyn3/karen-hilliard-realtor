Selling a luxury lake home is a different job from selling a luxury home inland. Buyers are paying for the house *and* the water. Here's how I approach it. For the first steps, read [what waterfront sellers should do first](/blog/selling-your-lake-lanier-home/).

**Price the whole property.** A lake home's value is bound up in its cove, water depth, dock, and community. Comparable sales need to match on those features, not just on bedrooms and square footage. I build pricing from the sales that truly compare to yours.

**Document the dock.** Permit, size, lift capacity, and condition are selling points when they're documented and problems when they aren't. See my [dock permit questions](/blog/lake-lanier-dock-permits-part-two/).

**Show the water.** Luxury lake buyers often come from out of state. Drone photography, water-level views, and video from the dock and approach by boat do the work of the first showing.

**Offer discretion when you want it.** Not every seller wants a big public launch. For luxury properties, options range from full marketing to a quieter approach, depending on your goals. See my [luxury homes page](/luxury-homes.html).

**Choose the window.** Spring shows the lake at its fullest, and winter buyers tend to be fewer and more serious. I'd rather choose the timing together based on your situation.

**Prepare the property.** Dock repairs, landscaping that frames the view, and clean sightlines to the water all pay off. Most of this can be done in the weeks before listing.

**Know your buyer.** Many luxury lake buyers are relocating or buying a second home. My [relocation guide](/blog/relocating-to-north-georgia/) explains how remote buyers shop, which shapes how your listing should be presented.
