Lake Lanier has several luxury communities, and they are not interchangeable. Here's how I think about them when I help buyers narrow the field. Each has its own detailed guide, linked below.

**Gated marina communities near Gainesville**
- [**Harbour Point**](/harbour-point-lake-lanier.html): a gated yacht-club community with marina slips and amenities. Good for boaters who want a built-in marina community.
- [**Marina Bay**](/marina-bay-lake-lanier.html): gated luxury with a private marina and craftsman-style architecture.
- [**Cumberland on Lanier**](/cumberland-on-lanier.html): a gated lake community minutes from Gainesville.

**Classic club-and-lake living**
- [**Chattahoochee Country Club**](/chattahoochee-country-club-lake-lanier.html): Gainesville's established club-and-lake address, with private docks.

**North-lake and deep-water enclaves**
- [**St. Michael's Bay**](/st-michaels-bay-lake-lanier.html): gated estate homes with a community marina on the lake's deep-water north end.
- [**The Reserve Club & Marina**](/reserve-club-lake-lanier.html): a gated north-lake community near Dawsonville with a private marina and custom homes.

**Forsyth County**
- [**Sidney on Lanier**](/sidney-on-lanier.html): a luxury enclave north of Browns Bridge, with estate homes and Port Royale boating.

**Golf and lake**
- [**Chestatee**](/chestatee-lake-lanier.html): Dawsonville's golf-and-lake community, with a course, marina access, and a mountain-side setting.

**How to choose.** Start with three questions:

1. *How will you use the lake?* Daily boating, entertaining, fishing, or mostly the view?
2. *How far are you willing to drive?* The south end is closer to Atlanta and GA-400. The north end is quieter and more remote.
3. *What do you want from the community?* Marina, golf, gates, or simply a private lot.

Every community has fees, rules, and dock guidelines worth understanding before you commit, and I walk through them with each buyer.
