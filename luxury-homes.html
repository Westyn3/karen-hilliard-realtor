How Karen Works

Luxury Homes,
The Way a Local Sees It

North Georgia's luxury market is not one market. It's several: the equestrian estates and gated golf communities of Milton and Alpharetta; Cumming's growing seven-figure market in South Forsyth and on the lake; deep-water Lake Lanier estates on the Gainesville and Cumming shores; and the luxury mountain homes of Blue Ridge, Dahlonega, and Big Canoe. Each has its own buyers, its own comparables, and its own due diligence. Karen works across all of them.

For sellers, luxury marketing is the difference. Karen's listings receive professional architectural and drone photography, cinematic video, staging guidance, and targeted exposure to her luxury and relocation network and to the out-of-state buyers who increasingly drive the top of this market. Pricing is built on true luxury comparables, and every showing is handled personally.

For buyers, Karen's construction management background is a genuine advantage at this price point. Estates come with barns, pools, outbuildings, complex mechanical systems, wells and septic, and docks — and she evaluates them properly before her clients commit. Whether you're buying your primary residence, a lake estate, or a mountain retreat, you'll have an agent who knows both the market and the property.

Good to Know

Luxury Homes
Buyer Questions

Where does the luxury market start in North Georgia?

Generally around $1M in Alpharetta, Cumming, and on Lake Lanier, and around $1.5M for true Milton estates. Mountain luxury in Blue Ridge and Dahlonega typically begins near $1M for long-range-view homes.

How do you market a luxury listing?

Professional architectural and drone photography, cinematic video, staging guidance, a pricing strategy built on luxury comparables, and targeted exposure to Karen's luxury buyer network, relocation contacts, and out-of-state buyers. Every showing is handled personally.

Do you work with out-of-state luxury buyers?

Constantly. Many of North Georgia's luxury buyers come from out of state, and Karen coordinates video tours, remote due diligence, and closings for clients who can't be here for every step.

What's different about due diligence on an estate?

Everything scales. Wells and septic sized for the home, pool and outbuilding permits, dock permits on the lake, barn and pasture condition on equestrian properties, and mechanical systems that a standard inspection may not fully cover. Karen's construction background makes this her strength.
