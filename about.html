Our Story

Meet
Karen Hilliard

A Lake Lanier resident, a construction professional turned Realtor®, and co-founder of The Legacy Team — Karen brings North Georgia buyers and sellers the local knowledge and hands-on expertise that luxury, lakefront, and mountain properties require.

The Agent

Karen Hilliard

Karen has called Lake Lanier home for more than twelve years, and that's where her real estate practice began. Today she leads The Legacy Team's luxury, waterfront, mountain, and land markets across North Georgia — from estates in Milton and Alpharetta to deep-water homes on Lanier and cabins in Blue Ridge — as a licensed Realtor® with Keller Williams North Atlanta.

Before real estate, Karen worked in construction management, and it shows. She reads a lot the way a builder does, walks new construction at every stage, and evaluates estates, docks, wells, and septic systems the way they need to be evaluated before her clients commit. She is licensed in both Georgia and Florida, where she also serves buyers and sellers along the Emerald Coast in Destin. Clients describe her as direct, thorough, and completely invested — the reason she holds a perfect 5.0 rating across 99 Google reviews.

  • Construction management background — new builds, land & estates
  • Luxury, lakefront, mountain cabin, multifamily & commercial experience
  • Licensed in Georgia and Florida

The Legacy Team

Britney Javens

Karen's daughter and teammate, Britney Javens, leads The Legacy Team's North Atlanta markets — Alpharetta, Milton, Roswell, Johns Creek, and the intown corridor from Buckhead to Brookhaven. Together they cover North Georgia from the Perimeter to the mountains, and every client gets both of them.
