Living in Alpharetta

Alpharetta,
The Way a Local Sees It

Alpharetta is the economic center of North Atlanta. Home to one of the largest concentrations of technology companies in the Southeast, it draws professionals and relocating families who want a short commute, excellent schools, and the walkable amenities of Avalon and downtown Alpharetta's City Center. GA-400 connects the city to Buckhead in one direction and Cumming and Lake Lanier in the other.

The housing market reflects that demand. Windward, built around a private lake with golf, tennis, and a marina, remains the city's signature luxury community. Downtown Alpharetta has added luxury townhomes and new construction within walking distance of restaurants. Established swim-tennis neighborhoods throughout the city feed top-rated Fulton County schools, and the luxury market along the Milton border runs well into seven figures.

Karen is based at Keller Williams North Atlanta on North Point Parkway, and Alpharetta is where she helps many clients decide where in North Georgia they belong — whether that's a luxury home here, an estate in Milton, more house in Cumming, or a lake home on Lanier. For sellers, she brings luxury marketing and a buyer network that reaches beyond the immediate area.

Good to Know

Alpharetta
Buyer Questions

How much do homes cost in Alpharetta, GA?

Townhomes and condos from the $400s, established single-family homes largely in the $600s–$1M range, and luxury homes in Windward, downtown, and along the Milton border from $1M well upward.

What are Alpharetta's best luxury neighborhoods?

Windward is the benchmark, with lake, golf, and marina amenities. Downtown Alpharetta's newer luxury construction and the estate communities along the Milton border round out the top of the market.

How are Alpharetta schools?

Fulton County's Alpharetta, Milton, and Cambridge high school clusters are consistently among the top-rated in Georgia. Zoning varies by neighborhood and is verified for every home.

Is Alpharetta a good base for exploring North Georgia?

It's the ideal one. GA-400 puts Cumming, Dawsonville, and Lake Lanier's southwest shore within 20–40 minutes, and Karen frequently helps Alpharetta clients compare in-town options against lake and mountain properties.
